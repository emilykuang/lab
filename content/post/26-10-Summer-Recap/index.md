This summer brought new faces, fresh ideas, and plenty of collaboration. We welcomed several undergraduate research assistants, shared our work, and celebrated two new publications:

- [**“I don’t want to engineer prompts, I want to engineer products”: 3D CAD Designers’ Views on Futuristic GenAI Concepts**](/lab/publication/tochi26-aiforcad)
- [**Hands-Off or Hands-On? Variation in Area Chair Practices and Implications for AI Support**](/lab/publication/aies26-areachairs)

Together, these papers reflect our work in human-AI collaboration, AI-supported creative work, and thoughtful, human-centered design.

We also got out into the community and hosted activities that made room for learning and connection:

#### CG Connect
The lab headed to CG Connect, hosted by Autodesk Research in Toronto, to meet the wider graphics and interactive systems community. [Read more here](https://lnkd.in/p/g9YftM48)

#### GI Conference
Pengqi Wang presented a demo and position paper on AI proactivity at the GI conference in Waterloo. [Read more here](https://lnkd.in/p/gw3BZ3Ki)

#### Lunch & Learn
The lab hosted a Lunch & Learn to share ideas and connect with Dr. Sharan from the University of Hamburg. [Read more here](https://lnkd.in/p/gmiA_Ga2)

#### CAIS Conference
Many of our undergraduate research assistants attended their first conference and presented posters at CAIS, learning from others in AI and HCI and connecting with the research community. [Read more here](https://lnkd.in/p/gCYbBT7c)

#### Bringing STEM to Life Symposium
Dr. Kuang joined the Bringing STEM to Life Symposium to see high school students’ projects addressing her research challenge: using AI to make education more accessible. [Read more here](https://lnkd.in/p/gbrnqR4b)

Thanks to everyone who made it such a lively and productive season!
